# What to Do When Your Session Expires

## "Your session has expired. Please sign in again."

If you see this message, don't worry! It's normal and easy to fix.

---

## Why This Happens

For your security, SuppliWise automatically logs you out after a period of time. This protects your health information if you:
- Leave your device unattended
- Haven't used the app in a while
- Close your browser and come back later

---

## Quick Fix (2 Steps)

### Step 1: Go to Login Page
- Click the **Login** button in the navigation bar
- Or go directly to the login page

### Step 2: Sign In Again
- Enter your **email** and **password**
- Click **Sign In**

**That's it!** You're back in and can continue where you left off.

---

## What Happens to Your Data?

### ✅ Your Data is Safe
- All your assessments are saved in your account
- Your health information is still there
- Nothing is lost when you're logged out

### ✅ Your History is Preserved
- View all your past assessments in the History page
- All recommendations are still available
- Nothing is deleted

---

## Tips to Avoid Frequent Logouts

1. **Use the app regularly** - Sessions stay active while you're using it
2. **Don't close the browser** - Keep the tab open if working on something
3. **Bookmark your pages** - Easy to get back after logging in
4. **Save your password** - Use your browser's password manager for quick login

## When You Might See This

### Common Scenarios
- ✓ You left the app open for several days
- ✓ Your computer went to sleep
- ✓ You cleared your browser data
- ✓ You switched browsers or devices
- ✓ You logged in from another device

---

## Different Ways to Sign Back In

### Option 1: Click Logout → Login
1. If you see a logout button, click it
2. You'll be redirected to the login page
3. Enter your credentials

### Option 2: Go Directly to Login
1. Navigate to the Login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click Sign In

### Option 3: Browser Console (Advanced)
If the page seems stuck:
1. Press **F12** (Windows) or **Cmd+Option+I** (Mac)
2. Click **Console** tab
3. Type: `localStorage.clear(); window.location.href = '/login';`
4. Press **Enter**
5. Sign in normally

---

## Troubleshooting

### "I don't remember my password"
- Use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page
- Follow the instructions to reset your password
- Check your email for the reset link

### "Login page won't load"
- **Refresh the page** (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- **Clear browser cache**:
  - Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Clear browsing data
  - Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Clear recent history
- Try a different browser
- Check your internet connection

### "I keep getting logged out"
This usually means:
1. **Token expired** - Normal after 7 days
2. **Browser clearing cookies** - Check browser settings
3. **Multiple devices** - Logging in elsewhere logs you out here
4. **Server restart** - Temporary, just log back in
