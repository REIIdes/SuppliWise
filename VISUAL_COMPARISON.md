## What's Different?

### Summary Section

**Detailed:** 80 words, medical terminology, demographic info, clinical language
**Simplified:** 12 words, plain language, just the benefit

### Individual Recommendations

#### REMOVED in Simplified:
1. ❌ "Recommended for" badge (shows conditions/symptoms)
2. ❌ "Based on your..." callout (references assessment)  
3. ❌ Detailed mechanism explanation (ATP synthesis, GABA, etc.)
4. ❌ Scientific citations with journal names and PMIDs
5. ❌ Side effects section (too medical/scary)
6. ❌ Interaction warnings (kept in basic info though)

#### SIMPLIFIED in Simplified:
1. ✅ Reason: From 60 words → 10 words
2. ✅ Evidence: From 50 words → 6 words
3. ✅ Evidence styling: Blue prominent → Gray subtle

#### KEPT in Both:
- ✅ Supplement name and form
- ✅ Priority badge
- ✅ Confidence score
- ✅ Dosage
- ✅ Timing
- ✅ Food sources

## Readability Comparison

### Detailed Mode Text Sample
> "Because you reported moderate fatigue and difficulty falling asleep, Magnesium Glycinate may help by supporting ATP synthesis for cellular energy, reducing neuromuscular excitability, and regulating GABA neurotransmission for better sleep quality."

**Analysis:**
- Reading level: College/Medical
- Complexity: High
- Technical terms: 7 (ATP, synthesis, neuromuscular, excitability, GABA, neurotransmission, moderate)
- Target: Healthcare professionals

### Simplified Mode Text Sample
> "Helps you relax, sleep better, and feel less tired."

**Analysis:**
- Reading level: 5th grade
- Complexity: Very low
- Technical terms: 0
- Target: Everyone

---

## Screen Space Comparison

### Detailed Mode
- Height per recommendation: ~500-600px
- Visible recommendations (1080p screen): 2-3
- Scroll required: Yes, frequently

### Simplified Mode
- Height per recommendation: ~250-300px
- Visible recommendations (1080p screen): 4-6
- Scroll required: Less frequent

**Result:** Simplified mode shows 2x more recommendations on screen, reducing scrolling.

---

## Visual Hierarchy

### Detailed Mode Emphasis
1. 🔴 Priority (most prominent)
2. 📊 Confidence score (second)
3. 🏷️ "Recommended for" badge (third)
4. 📝 Detailed reason (fourth)
5. 📚 Evidence (prominent blue box)

### Simplified Mode Emphasis
1. 🔴 Priority (most prominent)
2. 📊 Confidence score (second)
3. 📝 Simple reason (third)
4. 💊 Dosage/timing (fourth)
5. ✓ Evidence (subtle gray text)

**Result:** Simplified mode puts focus on action (dosage/timing) over explanation.

---

## Color Psychology

### Detailed Mode Colors
- **Blue** (Evidence) = Authority, trust, clinical
- **Green** (Food) = Health, natural
- **Orange** (Side effects) = Caution, attention

### Simplified Mode Colors
- **Gray** (Evidence) = Subtle, supportive, non-threatening
- **Green** (Food) = Health, natural (kept)
- **Orange** (Side effects) = Hidden

**Result:** Simplified mode is visually calmer, less overwhelming.

---

## User Experience Flow

### Detailed Mode Journey
1. See summary with medical terms
2. Scroll to recommendation
3. Read "Recommended for" → understand condition link
4. Read "Based on your..." → understand personal context
5. Read detailed reason → understand mechanism
6. Click "More details"
7. Read scientific evidence → verify claims
8. Read side effects → assess risks
9. Decide to take or not

**Time:** 3-5 minutes per recommendation
**Cognitive load:** High

### Simplified Mode Journey
1. See simple summary
2. Scroll to recommendation  
3. Read benefit in one glance
4. Check dosage and timing
5. Decide to take or not

**Time:** 30-60 seconds per recommendation
**Cognitive load:** Low

## Accessibility

### Detailed Mode
- Screen reader: ~2-3 minutes per recommendation
- Reading time: 3-5 minutes per recommendation  
- Cognitive load: High
- Best for: Educated users, healthcare professionals

### Simplified Mode
- Screen reader: ~30 seconds per recommendation
- Reading time: 30-60 seconds per recommendation
- Cognitive load: Low
- Best for: Everyone, especially elderly, ESL, low literacy

---

## Print/Export Comparison

### Detailed Mode PDF
- Pages per 20 recommendations: ~15-20 pages
- Suitable for: Doctor visits, medical records
- Information density: High

### Simplified Mode PDF
- Pages per 20 recommendations: ~5-7 pages
- Suitable for: Personal reference, sharing with family
- Information density: Optimal for scanning

## Which Mode Should Users Choose?

### Choose Detailed If You:
- ✓ Work in healthcare
- ✓ Want to verify scientific backing
- ✓ Plan to discuss with your doctor
- ✓ Like understanding mechanisms
- ✓ Have medical education

### Choose Simplified If You:
- ✓ Just want to know what to take
- ✓ Find medical terms confusing
- ✓ Want quick, easy guidance
- ✓ Feel overwhelmed by health info
- ✓ Prefer simple language
