# Understanding Your Recommendations: Simplified vs Detailed Mode

## What's New?

We've heard your feedback! Many users found the medical terminology and detailed scientific information overwhelming. That's why we've added **two viewing modes** for your supplement recommendations:

### 🌟 Simplified Mode (Default)
**Perfect for:** Most users who want clear, actionable advice without medical jargon

**What you'll see:**
- Easy-to-understand explanations in plain language
- Focus on how supplements help your daily life
- Friendly, encouraging tone
- Simple benefit descriptions

**Example:**
> "This can help you feel less tired during the day and have more energy for your activities. Many people notice improvements within 2-4 weeks."

---

### 📚 Detailed Mode
**Perfect for:** Healthcare professionals, biology students, or anyone who wants the full scientific picture

**What you'll see:**
- Professional medical terminology
- Scientific study citations with PMIDs
- Detailed mechanism of action
- Clinical-grade information

**Example:**
> "Because you reported chronic fatigue with moderate severity, Magnesium Glycinate may help by supporting ATP synthesis and reducing neuromuscular excitability. Ferracioli-Oda et al. (2013) PLOS ONE meta-analysis (PMID: 23691095)."

---

## How to Switch Modes

1. After completing your assessment, look for the toggle at the top of your results page
2. You'll see two buttons: **Simplified** and **Detailed**
3. Click the mode you prefer
4. Your choice is automatically saved and will be remembered next time!

---

## What Information Changes?

### Summary Section
- **Simplified**: Warm, encouraging overview of your plan
- **Detailed**: Clinical case summary with medical terminology

### Each Recommendation
- **Simplified**: "Why This Helps You" in everyday language
- **Detailed**: "Clinical Rationale" with mechanisms and conditions

### Evidence Section (under "More Details")
- **Simplified**: Simple explanation like "Studies show this helps..."
- **Detailed**: Full citations with journal names, authors, years, and PMID numbers

### What Stays the Same?
These are always shown regardless of mode:
- Supplement names and forms
- Dosage instructions
- Timing recommendations
- Food sources
- Interactions and warnings
- Side effects

---

## Which Mode Should You Choose?

### Choose Simplified if you:
- Want quick, easy-to-understand guidance
- Feel overwhelmed by medical terms
- Just want to know "what helps and why"
- Prefer practical, everyday language

### Choose Detailed if you:
- Are a healthcare professional or student
- Want to verify scientific backing
- Need detailed mechanism information
- Plan to discuss with your doctor using clinical terminology
- Like diving deep into the research

---

## Tips for Both Modes

1. **Start with Simplified**: Even if you're comfortable with medical terms, the simplified version is great for a quick overview
2. **Switch Anytime**: You can toggle between modes as often as you like - your results don't change, just how they're explained
3. **Print or Export**: When exporting to PDF, the current mode you've selected will be used
4. **Share with Others**: Send the simplified version to family members, detailed version to your doctor

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will my preference be saved?**
A: Yes! Your choice is saved in your browser and will be remembered when you return.

**Q: Can I switch modes for individual recommendations?**
A: Currently, the mode applies to all recommendations. This keeps the experience consistent and easy to understand.

**Q: Does this affect the quality of recommendations?**
A: Not at all! The underlying recommendations are identical - only the explanation style changes.

**Q: What if I'm sharing results with my doctor?**
A: Switch to Detailed mode before exporting or printing. Your doctor will appreciate the clinical terminology and study references.

**Q: I'm not seeing simplified text for some recommendations**
A: If you're viewing an older assessment (before this feature was added), some recommendations may only have the detailed version. Take a new assessment to get both versions!
